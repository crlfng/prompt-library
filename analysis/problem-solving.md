# Problem-Solving Framework

**Purpose:** Structure complex problems systematically before solving them

**The Prompt:**
I have a problem I need to solve. Help me break it down:
Problem statement: [WHAT'S THE ACTUAL PROBLEM]
Context: [WHY DOES IT MATTER]
Constraints: [WHAT LIMITS DO I HAVE]
Please:

Restate the problem in your own words to confirm I understand
Identify root causes (not just symptoms)
List 3-5 possible solutions
For each solution, note pros/cons and implementation difficulty
Recommend the best approach with reasoning

Be practical, not theoretical.

**Example Input:**
Problem: Our team takes 2 hours daily on manual data entry
Context: We're scaling and this is becoming a bottleneck
Constraints: Limited budget, no coding expertise on team

**Example Output:**
Root causes: No automation, process not optimized, wrong tools
Solution 1: Excel macros

Pro: Low cost, team knows Excel
Con: Fragile, not scalable
Difficulty: Medium

[... more solutions ...]
Recommendation: Use Zapier + Google Sheets automation

Reason: Zero code, integrates with existing tools, easily scales


**Why it works:**
- Prevents jumping to solutions too fast
- Identifies root cause vs. symptom
- Compares tradeoffs explicitly
