# Professional Email Draft

**Purpose:** Quickly draft professional business emails with appropriate tone, structure, and detail level

**The Prompt:**
You are an expert business communicator. I need to draft a professional email with these parameters:
Context: [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION]
Recipient: [WHO ARE YOU WRITING TO]
Key message: [WHAT'S THE MAIN POINT]
Tone: [FORMAL / FRIENDLY-PROFESSIONAL / URGENT]
Length: [BRIEF 3-5 LINES / MEDIUM 1 PARAGRAPH / DETAILED 2-3 PARAGRAPHS]
Write the email subject line and body. Use professional but warm language. Be direct in the first sentence.

**Example Input:**
Context: We've delayed a product launch by 2 weeks due to supply chain issues
Recipient: Client who was expecting delivery this week
Key message: Apologize, explain briefly, provide new timeline, reassure about quality
Tone: Friendly-professional
Length: Medium 1 paragraph

**Example Output:**
Subject: Launch Timeline Update - Still on Track for Quality
Hi [Client Name],
I wanted to personally update you on our product launch. We've pushed our delivery date to [DATE] to ensure we meet our quality standards — this gives us time to address some supply chain adjustments we encountered. I know this wasn't the timeline we originally discussed, and I appreciate your flexibility. The extra time means you'll receive a product that exceeds expectations. I'll send you a detailed delivery schedule by [DATE].
Best regards,
[Your Name]

**Why it works:**
- Opens with context and apology (acknowledges the situation)
- Gives a concrete new date (removes ambiguity)
- Reframes delay as quality assurance (maintains trust)
- Provides next step (shows ownership)
- Warm but professional tone (maintains relationship)

**Variations:**
- For internal stakeholders: increase formality, add metrics
- For difficult clients: add 1-2 sentences of appreciation for their patience
- For urgent situations: lead with action taken, not apology
