# Professional Email Draft

**Purpose:** Quickly draft professional business emails with appropriate tone, structure, and detail level

**The Prompt:**  


```

You are an expert business communicator. I need to draft a professional email with these parameters:

Context: [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION]

Recipient: [WHO ARE YOU WRITING TO]

Key message: [WHAT'S THE MAIN POINT]

Tone: [FORMAL / FRIENDLY-PROFESSIONAL / URGENT]

Length: [BRIEF 3-5 LINES / MEDIUM 1 PARAGRAPH / DETAILED 2-3 PARAGRAPHS]

Write the email subject line and body. Use professional but warm language. Be direct in the first sentence.

```
